Company Story & Origin

Crafted by Master Peelers in Southern Sri Lanka

**Jade Cinnamon Lanka** was established with a singular mission: to supply the world’s most exquisite, unadulterated Ceylon Cinnamon directly from origin plantations to global food manufacturers, extractors, and spice houses.

Authentic Ceylon Cinnamon cannot be machine-fabricated. It requires generational peeling artisans who scrape the outer bark, delicately extract the parchment-thin inner layer, and hand-roll up to 50 concentric layers into cigar-like quills. We partner directly with smallholder farmer cooperatives across Galle, Matara, and Ratnapura, guaranteeing fair-trade pricing and pristine traceability.
